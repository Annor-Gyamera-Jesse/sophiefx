The Story

A note from Sophie.

I started trading in 2019 with the same misconceptions everyone else does — that the market would make me rich quickly, that the secret was finding the perfect indicator, that more screen time meant more money. I lost three accounts learning the truth.

What changed was meeting a mentor of my own. Someone who didn't sell signals or promise yachts. Someone who simply showed me, week after week, what disciplined trading actually looks like — the journals, the missed trades, the sitting on hands. The boring parts.

That's what I teach now. The boring parts. Because they are the only parts that ever made me money.

If you're tired of YouTube gurus and Telegram signals — if you want a mentor who will look at your journal, ask hard questions, and call you when you go off-plan — that's what this is. Welcome.

The Curriculum

Six weeks. One discipline.

A structured, progressive curriculum built around one idea: making you independent.

WK 1
Foundation & Audit

We open your trading journal together. Honest accounting of wins, losses, and the patterns hiding in both.

WK 2
Market Structure

How price actually moves — highs, lows, breaks of structure, change of character. The vocabulary pros speak.

WK 3
Smart Money Concepts

Liquidity, order blocks, fair value gaps. Where institutions place orders — and how to position yourself.

WK 4
Risk Engineering

Position sizing as a function of conviction. Stop placement that respects structure, not fear.

WK 5
Live Sessions

We trade together. London open. New York open. You watch me think. I watch you think.

WK 6
Independence Protocol

You graduate with your own playbook, rules, and journal practice. The point was always you, ready.
